OTP BankBanking · public websiteStudio · B2C2024

Task

Refresh two pages of the bank’s public website: the OTP debit card and the compliance page, which isn’t a client page but has to be on the site by regulation.

What I did

  • Debit card page: set the order of blocks based on research the bank ran and shared with us
  • Reworked the feedback form the bank tracked a satisfaction score with: on mobile the 10-point scale merged into a row of tiny numbers and caused misclicks, so I moved it to a 5-point scale with tap-friendly buttons
  • Added a comment field for scores below 4, so an unhappy user can say what exactly is wrong and the team can act on it
  • Compliance page: turned old pages and a pile of technical documentation into a readable structure
  • The 3D illustrations were later replaced by another team; the structure and blocks are mine
